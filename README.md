1. Introduction
Customer Churn and Sales Trend Analysis is a Data Science project that focuses on understanding customer behavior, identifying customer loss, and analyzing business sales performance. It helps companies understand why customers stop using their products or services and how sales change over a specific period.

By analyzing customer and sales datasets using Python, businesses can identify important patterns, improve customer satisfaction, and increase revenue.

2. Project Description
In this project, we use Python libraries to analyze customer information, purchase history, sales records, revenue, and customer retention. We perform data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualization to identify customer churn patterns and sales trends.

The project has two main parts:
- Customer Churn Analysis: Identifies customers who have stopped using a company's services and examines possible reasons for leaving.
- Sales Trend Analysis: Studies sales performance over days, months, and years to identify growth, decline, popular products, and revenue patterns.

3. Objectives
- To analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- To identify customers who leave a company.
- To calculate customer churn percentage.
- To understand customer retention rates.
- To analyze monthly and yearly sales performance.
- To identify the best-selling products.
- To compare revenue across different periods.
- To discover patterns and trends in business data.
- To help businesses make better decisions.
